SLIDE 1: My approach | 0:00–0:38
My part is off-market prediction: estimating a home's sale price without its asking price. I used histogram gradient boosting to learn log sale prices. Learning log prices reduces the influence of very expensive homes. The model captures nonlinear relationships between a home's characteristics and price. It combines size, rooms, parking, property type, synthetic location, listing date, and description cues. It excludes list price and post-sale information. Missing measurements remain unknown, and category encoders learn only from the training fold.
